# UtilDeck Privacy Policy

UtilDeck does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- All tools run locally in your browser. The text, passwords, tokens, files and numbers you enter stay on your device.
- The extension requests no browser permissions and makes no network requests.
- Your theme choice and Pomodoro settings are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and can be cleared from the browser's site data settings.
- The QR code and password generator use your browser's built-in random and drawing functions. No external service is involved.
