Sitemap Generator

Paste one address per line, choose optional settings, and get a sitemap.xml to upload to your site. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How to use Sitemap Generator

  1. Paste one full address per line.
  2. Add an optional last-modified date, change frequency and priority.
  3. Download sitemap.xml and upload it to your site root.

Where should sitemap.xml go?

At the root of your site, such as https://example.com/sitemap.xml.

Is there a limit?

A sitemap can list up to 50,000 URLs. Split larger sites into several files.
