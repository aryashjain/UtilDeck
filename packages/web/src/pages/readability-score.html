Readability Score

Check how easy your writing is to read. Shows the Flesch reading ease, a grade level, and long sentences to shorten. Runs in your browser.

How to use Readability Score

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Read the reading ease and grade level.
  3. Shorten the long sentences if the score is too low for your readers.

What is a good reading ease score?

Scores of 60 to 70 are plain English that most adults can read. Higher is easier.

How accurate is it?

It is an estimate. Syllables are counted with a simple rule, so it can be a little off for unusual words.
