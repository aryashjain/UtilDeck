Image Cropper

Drag on the image to select the area you want to keep. Pick an aspect ratio for social posts, thumbnails or banners. Cropping happens in your browser.

Crop & Download

How to use Image Cropper

  1. Choose or drop an image.
  2. Drag on the image to select the area to keep, or pick an aspect ratio such as 1:1 or 16:9.
  3. Download the cropped image.

Can I crop an image to a square?

Yes. Choose 1:1 in the aspect ratio list, then drag to place the square.

Does cropping lower the image quality?

Cropping keeps the original pixels in the area you select. Only the size changes.
