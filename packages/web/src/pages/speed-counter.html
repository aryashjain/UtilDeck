How to use Typing Speed

  1. Start the timed test and type the passage shown.
  2. Your words per minute (WPM) update as you type.
  3. Compare your result with the average, which is about 40 WPM.

What is a good typing speed?

About 40 WPM is average for most people. Typists who work all day often reach 60 WPM or more.

Does UtilDeck record what I type?

No. The test runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
