Merge PDF

Combine several PDF files into one, in the order you choose. Your files stay in your browser.

    How to use Merge PDF

    1. Add two or more PDF files.
    2. Arrange them with the arrows.
    3. Press Merge and download the combined PDF.

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Merging happens in your browser, so the files never leave your device.

    Why does a file fail to merge?

    Encrypted or damaged PDFs cannot be read. Remove the password or repair the file first.
