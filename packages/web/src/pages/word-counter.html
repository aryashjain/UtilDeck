How to use Word Counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the input box.
  2. Read the live counts for words, characters, sentences and paragraphs.
  3. Use Copy Stats to copy the numbers.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute, rounded up.

What is the difference between Chars and No Space?

Chars counts every character, including spaces. No Space leaves spaces out.
