GST Calculator

Work out GST on an amount, with the tax split into CGST and SGST for in-state sales or IGST for interstate sales. Amounts are in rupees; the rates are the standard GST slabs.

How to use GST Calculator

  1. Enter the amount and choose whether it includes GST.
  2. Choose the rate and the sale type.
  3. Read the tax, the split and the total.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

Within a state, GST splits equally into CGST and SGST. Between states, it is charged as IGST.

Are the rates current?

These are the standard GST slabs. Check the latest rates for your goods or services.
