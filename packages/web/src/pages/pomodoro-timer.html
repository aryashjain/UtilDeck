How to use Pomodoro Timer

  1. Press Start to begin a 25 minute focus session.
  2. Take the short break when it ends. After four focus sessions you get a long break.
  3. A sound plays at each change. Reset starts the count again.

What is the Pomodoro technique?

Working in focused 25 minute blocks, with short breaks between them and a longer break after four.

Why does the timer keep time when the tab is in the background?

It works from the end time rather than counting ticks, so it stays accurate even when the browser slows background tabs.
