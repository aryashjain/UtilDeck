How to use Case Converter

  1. Paste your text into the input box.
  2. Click the case you want, such as UPPERCASE, Title Case or snake_case.
  3. Copy the converted text from the result box.

What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word, for example "The Quick Brown Fox".

What is camelCase and snake_case?

camelCase joins words and capitalizes each word after the first. snake_case joins words with underscores, which is common in code.
