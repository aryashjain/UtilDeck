Split PDF

Pull out the pages you need into a new PDF. Enter pages such as 1-3, 5, or 8-end.

How to use Split PDF

  1. Choose a PDF and wait for its page count.
  2. Enter the pages to keep, such as 1-3, 5 or 8-end.
  3. Download the new PDF.

Can I split into one file per page?

Extract pages in a few runs, or use a range for each page you need.

Does splitting lower quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are.
