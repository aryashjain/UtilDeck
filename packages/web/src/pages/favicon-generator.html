Favicon Generator

Drop one square image (512 by 512 or larger works best). Get every icon a website needs: favicon.ico, PNG icons, the Apple touch icon, Android and app icons, and a manifest. Images are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Files

Put the files in the root folder of your site. The HTML tags go inside the head of each page.

How to use Favicon Generator

  1. Drop a square image, ideally 512 by 512 pixels or more.
  2. Check the icon sizes, then download the ones you need or favicon.ico.
  3. Put the files in your site's root folder and paste the HTML tags into each page's head.

Which favicon files do I need?

favicon.ico for older browsers, 32 and 16 pixel PNGs for tabs, a 180 pixel Apple touch icon, and 192 and 512 pixel icons with a manifest for installable apps.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Icons are made in your browser, so the image never leaves your device.
