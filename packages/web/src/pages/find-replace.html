Replace every match in your text. Turn on regular expressions to use patterns like \d+ or capture groups like $1.

How to use Find & Replace

  1. Paste your text into the input box.
  2. Type what to find and what to replace it with.
  3. Turn on Regular expression to match patterns, such as \d+ for numbers.

What is a regular expression?

A regular expression is a pattern for matching text. For example, \d+ matches one or more digits.

Can I replace text in a case-sensitive way?

Yes. Tick Match case to replace only the exact capitalization you typed.
