BMI Calculator

Enter your weight and height to get your body mass index and the standard weight category. BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis.

How to use BMI Calculator

  1. Enter your weight in kilograms and height in centimetres.
  2. Read your BMI and its weight category.

What is a healthy BMI?

Most adults fall in the 18.5 to 24.9 range. BMI does not measure body fat directly, so check with a professional.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always. Muscle adds weight, so BMI can overstate body fat for people who train hard.
