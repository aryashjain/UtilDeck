Bill Splitter

Enter the bill, the tip you want to leave and how many people are paying.

How to use Bill Splitter

  1. Enter the bill total.
  2. Enter the tip percentage.
  3. Enter how many people are paying.

Is the tip taken from the total?

The tip is added to the bill total, and the grand total is split equally.

Does it handle uneven splits?

This version splits evenly. Each person pays the same share.
