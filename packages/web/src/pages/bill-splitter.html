    How to use Expense Splitter

    1. Add everyone in the group.
    2. Add each expense, choose who paid, and split it equally, by percentage or by exact amounts.
    3. Read the balances and the payments listed under Settle up.

    How do I split a bill with friends?

    Add everyone, enter the bill, and choose who paid. The balances show what each person owes or is owed.

    Is this a Splitwise alternative?

    It covers the core of Splitwise: several people, equal, percentage and exact splits, and the fewest payments to settle up. Data stays in your browser and is not synced across devices.
