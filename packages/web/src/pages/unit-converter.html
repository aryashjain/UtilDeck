Unit Converter

Pick a group, then the units. Works for length, weight, volume, data size and temperature.

How to use Unit Converter

  1. Pick a group such as length or weight.
  2. Choose the units to convert from and to.
  3. Type a value to see the result instantly.

Can I convert between different groups, such as miles to kilograms?

No. Units are only converted within their group, so the tool refuses mixed groups.

Which temperature scales does it support?

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.
