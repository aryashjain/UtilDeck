How to use World Clock and Meeting Planner

  1. Pick the cities you want to compare.
  2. Choose a meeting date and your working hours.
  3. Read the UTC hours where everyone is inside working hours.

How are shared hours found?

For each UTC hour on the date, the tool checks whether every city is inside the working hours you set.

Does it account for daylight saving time?

Yes. Each city uses its own time zone rules for that date.
