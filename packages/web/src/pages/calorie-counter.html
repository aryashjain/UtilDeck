    How to use Calorie Counter

    1. Choose your sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal.
    2. Press Calculate to see your maintenance calories and daily target.
    3. Use the protein, carbs and fat numbers as a starting point for your meals.

    How many calories do I need a day?

    It depends on your body and activity. This calculator estimates maintenance using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then adjusts it for your goal.

    Is this calorie calculator accurate?

    It gives a useful estimate for most adults, but it is not medical advice. Adjust your intake based on how your weight changes over a few weeks.
