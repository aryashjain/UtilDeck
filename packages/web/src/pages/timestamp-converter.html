Timestamp Converter

Turn a Unix timestamp into a readable date, or a date into a timestamp. Times are shown in UTC.

How to use Timestamp Converter

  1. Type a Unix timestamp and choose seconds or milliseconds to see the date.
  2. Or type a date to get its seconds and milliseconds.
  3. Use Use current time to fill in now.

What is a Unix timestamp?

The number of seconds since 1 January 1970 in UTC. Some systems use milliseconds instead.

Which time zone is shown?

Results are shown in UTC, so they match across all devices.
