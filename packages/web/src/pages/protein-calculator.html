How to use Protein Calculator

  1. Enter your weight.
  2. Pick your goal.
  3. Read your daily protein target in grams.

How much protein do I need?

The general guideline is 0.8 g per kg. People who train or build muscle often need more.

Is this a medical recommendation?

No. Talk to a doctor or dietitian for a plan that fits your health.
