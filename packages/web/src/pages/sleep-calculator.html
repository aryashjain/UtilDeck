How to use Sleep Calculator

  1. Choose whether you want to wake up at a time or go to bed at a time.
  2. Enter the time and read the options.

What is a sleep cycle?

Sleep moves through stages in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle often feels easier.

Is this medical advice?

No. Everyone sleeps differently, so use this as a guide.
