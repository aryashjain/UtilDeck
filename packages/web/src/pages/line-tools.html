How to use Line Tools

  1. Paste one item per line.
  2. Sort the lines A to Z or Z to A, or reverse their order.
  3. Remove duplicate lines so each one appears once.

How do I remove duplicate lines online?

Paste your list, then use the remove duplicates option. Each line is kept once, in its original order.
