UUID Generator

Make random version 4 UUIDs, one or up to 1,000,000 at a time. Copy them (up to 10,000) or download a file. Made with the browser’s secure random source.

How to use UUID Generator

  1. Choose how many UUIDs you need, up to 1,000,000.
  2. Copy them if there are up to 10,000, or download a .txt file for more.

What is a UUID?

A 128-bit identifier written as 32 hex digits in five groups. Version 4 UUIDs are random, and collisions are practically impossible.

Are these UUIDs secure?

They come from the browser’s secure random source, so they are hard to guess. Still, use a server-side generator for production secrets.
