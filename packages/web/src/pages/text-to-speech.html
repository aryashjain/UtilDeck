Text to Speech

Paste text and listen to it read aloud, with the voices your browser or device provides. Nothing is sent to a server.

How to use Text to Speech

  1. Type or paste text.
  2. Pick a voice, and adjust the speed and pitch.
  3. Press Read aloud, and Stop to end it.

Why do the voices differ?

Voices come from your browser or device, so the list depends on what is installed.

Is the text sent to a server?

No. Speech is generated by your device.
