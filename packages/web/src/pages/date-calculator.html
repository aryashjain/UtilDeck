How to use Date Calculator

  1. Enter two dates as YYYY-MM-DD to see the calendar and working days between them.
  2. Enter a start date and a number of days to add or subtract.
  3. Pick a date and time for the countdown.

How are working days counted?

Monday to Friday. Weekends are skipped, and public holidays are not.

Does the time zone change the day count?

No. Dates are counted as calendar days, so a day never shifts.
