Water Intake Calculator

Estimate your daily water needs from your weight, plus extra for exercise. A rough guide, not a medical target.

How to use Water Intake

  1. Enter your weight.
  2. Enter how many minutes you exercised today.
  3. Read the daily water estimate.

How much water should I drink?

A common guide is about 35 ml per kg of body weight a day, plus extra for exercise and heat.

Does this include the water in food?

No. Food also adds water, so the estimate is a little high for most people.
