How to use Image Compressor

  1. Choose or drop a PNG, JPEG or WebP image.
  2. Lower the quality or set a maximum width to make the file smaller.
  3. Download the compressed image.

Which format makes the smallest file?

JPEG and WebP usually produce small files for photos. PNG keeps every pixel but is larger.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image is processed in your browser and never leaves your device.
