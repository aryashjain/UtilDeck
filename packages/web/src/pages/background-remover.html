Background Remover

Remove a plain, solid-colour background, such as a white or flat-coloured backdrop around a logo or product. The result is a PNG with a transparent background. Works in your browser; the picture is never uploaded. It does not handle busy photo backgrounds.

How to use Background Remover

  1. Drop an image with a plain background.
  2. Adjust the tolerance until only the background is transparent.
  3. Download the PNG.

Why does it leave some background behind?

It removes one solid colour, taken from the edges. Busy or shaded backgrounds need an AI-based remover.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, and the PNG is made on your device.
