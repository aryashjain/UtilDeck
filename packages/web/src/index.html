UtilDeck

Your everyday toolkit

A deck of tools, made for you

Count, clean, compare, convert and generate text. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded unless you create a share link.

We don't care about your data, because we don't have the compute to store it. XD (Share links are the one exception: they're kept for 3 hours, then deleted.)
  • Notepad: Autosaving notes in your browser, with live stats and shareable links.
  • Word Counter: Words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and reading time.
  • Typing Speed: Timed typing test that measures your words per minute.
  • Case Converter: UPPER, lower, Title, Sentence, camelCase, snake_case and more.
  • Space Remover: Remove extra spaces and line breaks in one click.
  • Line Tools: Sort, deduplicate and reverse lines of text.
  • Find & Replace: Replace text in bulk, with optional regular expressions.
  • Text Extractor: Pull emails, URLs, numbers and hashtags out of any text.
  • Text Diff: Compare two texts side by side and see what changed.
  • Encoder: Encode and decode Base64, URL and HTML entities.
  • JSON Formatter: Format, minify and validate JSON.
  • Generators: Lorem ipsum placeholder text and URL-friendly slugs.
  • Password Generator: Strong random passwords made with your browser’s secure random source.
  • Image Compressor: Shrink JPEG, WebP or PNG images with a quality slider. Runs in your browser.
  • Image Cropper: Crop images by dragging a box, with preset aspect ratios.
